Privacy note

Job Autopilot runs on your own computer. This online dashboard is an optional window into it, so you can check progress from your phone and start a run. Last updated October 23, 2026.

What is stored here

  • Your account: email, a securely hashed password (managed by Supabase Auth; nobody can read it), your display name, and whether you opted in to updates.
  • Progress summaries from your computer: counts (applied today, ready, interviews), today's greeting, what's running in the background, the last and next run time.
  • Job list metadata: company, job title, location, listed pay, status, match score, posting link, and dates.
  • Email-update summaries: company, role, the kind of update (received, rejected, assessment, interview, offer), a one-line summary, and any deadline.
  • Requests you send (Run now, Check email) and their status.

What never leaves your computer

  • Your resumes, cover letters, and master prompt.
  • The text of your emails, your email passwords, and your Claude account.
  • Your networking contacts and referral messages.

Who can see it

Only you, plus any other email addresses you add under Account → Other emails that can sign in and that accept the invitation (adding an address does nothing until whoever owns it signs in and accepts). Every table is protected by row-level security, so a signed-in account can read only its own rows and those of an owner who added its confirmed email. An added email can start runs but can't change your data, add emails, or connect computers; you can remove it at any time. The site's operator (Manas Nikte) can see the following, to support people and see how the app is used:

  • Your account: your name and email, any other sign-in emails you added, when you joined and last signed in to this site, whether you opted in to updates, and how many requests (Run now, Check email) you sent from the site.
  • Your computers: each computer's name, operating system, and Job Autopilot version, when it was connected, last seen, and last synced, and the city you chose to show (Settings → Online dashboard on your computer; leave it blank to keep it private).
  • Numbers only: how many applications you sent today, this week, in the last 14 days, and in total, plus applications per day for the last 14 days, how many jobs are ready, waiting on your decision, interviewing, rejected, or at each pipeline stage, assessments still to finish, items in Needs you, estimated hours saved, your role families (such as "Data Analyst") with their counts, and when your last run happened and the next one is scheduled.

The operator never sees which companies or jobs you apply to, job links, your assessments' or email updates' content, or your documents. If you checked the optional box, Manas may email you occasional updates about Job Autopilot. You can ask to stop at any time.

Your computer stays in control

Your computer connects out to this dashboard; nothing here can connect in. The dashboard can only ask for four things (run, process the queue, check email, resume after a usage limit), and your computer checks every request before acting. Your password is never stored on your computer; it keeps only a renewable sign-in token in the system keychain.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a computer (its Settings page) deletes that computer's data here. Delete my account on the dashboard removes your account and everything stored for it, immediately.
